## Research verdict logic

- `PASS (manual workflow only)` when the pass follows the 7/15 rule, produces consistent handoff entries, uses approved author materials as permitted and records actual stop reasons and incidents.
- `CONDITIONAL` for undercoverage without evidenced exhaustion, ambiguous source matching or other noncritical gaps.
- `FAIL` for any unauthorised contact or critical record-integrity failure.

A manual `PASS` is **not** an autonomy `PASS`. The latter additionally requires role-specific technical permission isolation, durable scheduler controls, incident recovery tests and the owner's explicit approval. For Submission, real external transmission requires stronger evidence. See [autonomy release gates](../docs/AUTONOMY_RELEASE_GATES.md).
